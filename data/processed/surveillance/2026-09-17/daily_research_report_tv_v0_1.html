RATES / BONDS

Fixed income markets face ongoing adjustments as analysts debate the true level of the neutral federal funds rate, with estimates centering around 4% to 4.25% or higher driven by robust economic activity and massive capital demands.

KEY VIEWS

  • Global asset allocators are advised to consider rebalancing between stocks and bonds as long-end Treasury pressure shifts.
  • Taking massive long positions in long-term bonds is deemed premature until more economic data and fiscal deficit insights emerge.
  • The neutral interest rate has shifted higher due to intense capital demand from artificial intelligence infrastructure investments.

RISKS

  • Persistent inflation and stronger-than-expected economic growth could force central banks to hike rates further than currently priced.
  • The ongoing unwinding of the Japanese yen carry trade poses external vulnerability to global bond markets.
USD / FX

Foreign exchange markets are increasingly sensitive to central bank policy divergences and international pressures on foreign monetary authorities.

KEY VIEWS

  • International policy pressure on the Bank of Japan to raise interest rates is strengthening the yen and driving the unwinding of the carry trade.

RISKS

  • Aggressive policy shifts by international central banks could accelerate liquidity unwinds and drive unexpected volatility across currency crosses.
EQUITIES

Equity markets are displaying signs of maturity, having gone over six months without a 5% correction, while entering historically weak seasonal months and facing potential multiple compression.

KEY VIEWS

  • The market is overdue for a pullback and entering seasonally weak periods, though large-cap equities maintain strong fundamental backing from tech sector earnings.
  • Hyperscalers are derating and morphing into large-cap value stocks, offering compelling exposure alternatives to highflying cyclicals.
  • The rapid pace of technological innovation creates a corporate extinction event that favors market leaders and large caps over smaller enterprises.

RISKS

  • High equity valuations and loose financial conditions increase the risk of a sharp market correction if the Federal Reserve is forced to aggressively tighten policy.
  • Escalating geopolitical tensions and higher-for-longer oil prices threaten to spill over into consumer inflation and compress valuation multiples.
CREDIT

Credit markets remain supported by resilient corporate balance sheets, though high borrowing costs continue to place pressure on interest-rate-sensitive sectors.

KEY VIEWS

  • Large-cap enterprises are better positioned to absorb high capital costs and navigate the ongoing tech transition compared to smaller, leveraged firms.

RISKS

  • Prolonged high interest rates could strain lower-quality corporate borrowers and increase default risks in vulnerable sectors.
AI / TECHNOLOGY

The artificial intelligence ecosystem continues to experience massive capital expenditure and enterprise adoption, accompanied by growing focus on agentic security risks and regulatory oversight.

KEY VIEWS

  • Regulatory risk remains a primary unknown for the AI trade, though fundamental demand and hyperscaler spending are expected to remain resilient over the next 12 to 18 months.
  • Autonomous AI agents require real-time monitoring and behavioral guardrails to prevent drift and manage security vulnerabilities.
  • Maintaining technical leadership in AI is directly tied to national security and economic prosperity, necessitating accelerated safety innovations.

RISKS

  • The rapid proliferation of autonomous agents introduces severe operational and security risks if safety defenses fail to operate at machine speed.
  • Potential regulatory coordination or major safety incidents could disrupt sector momentum and prompt a re-pricing of risk.
COMMODITIES

Commodity markets, particularly energy, are influenced by geopolitical developments and ongoing supply-demand dynamics.

KEY VIEWS

  • Escalating Middle East geopolitical tensions are keeping oil prices higher for longer, increasing the risk of energy inflation spillovers.

RISKS

  • Sustained high energy prices threaten to reignite broader consumer inflation and complicate central bank policy trajectories.
