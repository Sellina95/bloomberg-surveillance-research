## Rates / Bonds

### Summary

Fixed-income markets face upward pressure on long-term yields due to persistent fiscal deficits, heavy hyperscaler bond issuance, and sticky inflation, though high-quality bonds and corporate credit offer attractive yields.

### Key Views

- Interest rates will remain higher for longer as front-end inflation lingers around 3.5% and long-end yields reflect heavy supply and fiscal expansion.
- The backup in bond yields is largely driven by market sentiment and policy transition confusion rather than economic overheating.
- Some Federal Reserve officials indicate that underlying demand and economic strength could justify maintaining or raising rates rather than easing.

### Risks

- Prolonged restrictive policy stance increasing debt-service burdens.
- Yield curve steepening driven by unaddressed fiscal deficit paths and sovereign debt issuance.


## USD / FX

### Summary

Foreign exchange markets navigate divergent global central bank paths and localized inflation dynamics, with cross-border trade friction and tariff shifts acting as swing factors.

### Key Views

- Central banks globally are splitting hairs on localized inflation trends without a single global macroeconomic event to dictate unison.
- Trade policy shifts and tariff adjustments with key partners like Canada introduce stress points for regional sectors and currencies.

### Risks

- Escalation of cross-border trade frictions and retaliatory counter-tariffs impacting growth.
- Divergent central bank communications causing unexpected currency volatility.


## Equities

### Summary

Equities remain supported by powerful earnings momentum in technology and AI hardware, though hidden leverage in footnotes and valuation concentrations prompt selective rotation into large-cap value.

### Key Views

- Nvidia's 70% revenue growth forecast and multi-quarter visibility reinforce institutional confidence in the hardware trade.
- Software and application layers are recovering as enterprises realize the necessity of deployment tools, alongside 25% of S&P 500 companies reporting margin benefits from AI.
- Large-cap value offers an appealing alternative for investors seeking inflation-protected income amid demographic shifts.

### Risks

- Opaque leverage and financing structures hiding true liabilities among tech hyperscalers.
- Near-term political and environmental pushback against data center expansions.

### Risks

- Deteriorating credit metrics for lower-income consumers exposed to restrictive interest rates.
