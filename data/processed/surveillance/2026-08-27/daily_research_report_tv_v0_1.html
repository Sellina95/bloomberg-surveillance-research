RATES / BONDS

Long-term interest rates face upward pressure from fiscal conditions, heavy hyperscaler bond issuance, and persistent stickiness in inflation rather than solely from monetary policy actions.

KEY VIEWS

  • High-quality corporate bonds and fixed-income yields offering 5% to 6% present attractive entry points for incremental capital.
  • Recent backups in bond yields reflect sentiment and confusion around central bank policy rather than economic overheating.
  • Long-term yields face structural upward pressure from heavy corporate and sovereign issuance.

RISKS

  • Failure by central bank leadership to clarify reaction functions could cause term premiums to drift higher.
  • Persistent fiscal deficits and heavy supply could overwhelm demand in the fixed-income market.
USD / FX

The U.S. dollar is supported by resilient labor market data and hawkish FOMC policy dialogue, though cross-border trade friction and regional economic divergence introduce volatility.

KEY VIEWS

  • Stable jobless claims remove employment-related hurdles for hawkish rate hikes, reinforcing dollar support.
  • Global central bank divergence creates localized currency pressures as countries face varying inflation dynamics.

RISKS

  • Escalating trade tensions and counter-tariffs from trade partners could dampen broader economic growth expectations.
EQUITIES

Equities remain driven by strong earnings momentum in mega-cap tech and AI infrastructure, though valuation concentrations and hidden leverage remain key structural concerns.

KEY VIEWS

  • Nvidia and the broader tech sector exhibit robust earnings momentum with significant visibility extending 12 to 18 months out.
  • Large-cap value stocks offer an attractive rotation opportunity for demographic groups seeking inflation-protected income.
  • Software and application-layer companies are regaining favor as enterprises deploy AI models into production.

RISKS

  • High equity concentration in mega-cap tech leaves portfolios vulnerable to sentiment shifts and potential financing shocks.
  • Data center expansion faces near-term political headwinds and public backlash ahead of midterms.
CREDIT

Corporate credit remains supported by strong cash flows among major tech players, though rising leverage and financing structures in supply chains require careful monitoring.

KEY VIEWS

  • High-quality corporate credit yields provide appealing carry for investors deploying incremental cash.
  • Hyperscaler leverage is increasingly tied to complex financing structures and footnotes rather than simple balance sheet debt.

RISKS

  • Lower-tier consumer credit shows strain with rising credit card delinquencies and tighter credit lines.
AI / TECHNOLOGY

AI adoption has transitioned from experimental interest to tangible fundamental impact, characterized by voracious demand for compute and expanding profit margins.

KEY VIEWS

  • Nvidia's 70% growth guidance is supported by major architecture shifts (Vera Rubin, Rubin Ultra) and active ecosystem financing.
  • Application-layer software companies are capturing renewed interest as enterprises require tools to interact with foundational models.
  • Data center buildouts are encountering localized pushback regarding noise, power consumption, and environmental impact.

RISKS

  • A discrepancy between reported earnings gains and actual cash flow generation in tech could compress valuation multiples if demand softens.
COMMODITIES

Data center expansion and AI infrastructure buildouts are driving localized energy and commodity price pressures.

KEY VIEWS

  • Power-intensive data centers are creating underlying inflation shocks in energy and power markets.
  • Cross-border trade of critical commodities like crude oil and electricity remains sensitive to bilateral trade tensions.

RISKS

  • Energy grid constraints and local permitting delays could bottleneck data center deployment schedules.
