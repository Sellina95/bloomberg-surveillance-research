## Rates / Bonds

### Summary

Fixed-income markets face ongoing uncertainty regarding inflation persistence and strong labor data, challenging the reliability of bonds as a portfolio diversifier.

## Equities

### Summary

Equity markets experienced heightened volatility following Nvidia's leadership transition, with investors re-evaluating single-stock concentration and rotating toward alternative tech leaders.

## AI / Technology

### Summary

The artificial intelligence ecosystem is moving past its initial directional buildout phase toward a selective, multi-vendor environment supported by multiyear hardware pipelines.

### Key Views

- While Jensen Huang's exit introduces short-term uncertainty, multiyear development cycles ensure product strategy remains stable over the medium term.

### Risks

- Executive departures and potential succession gaps impacting strategic execution and competitive moats.
- Intensified export controls and cloud compute access rules disrupting international data center deployments.

- **Market Impact:** Upward pressure on sovereign bond yields, tighter financial conditions, and reduced fixed-income diversification capacity.
