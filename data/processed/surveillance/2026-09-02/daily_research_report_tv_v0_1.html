COMMODITIES

Global commodity discussions center on acute vulnerability in petroleum markets, driven by heavily drawn-down strategic reserves and multi-season lows in distillate inventories.

KEY VIEWS

  • Using Venezuelan crude to refill the SPR is unrealistic due to extreme oil density, high viscosity, and a complete absence of domestic upgrading and hydro-treating capacity.
  • SPR import cover has halved from pre-crisis levels.
  • Commercial distillate inventories sit at historic lows for this point in the seasonal demand cycle.

RISKS

  • Inability to easily replenish emergency stockpiles leaves the economy exposed to supply shocks.
  • Vulnerability to sudden price spikes as seasonal demand transitions from weak periods to heavy agricultural, intermodal, and heating requirements.
